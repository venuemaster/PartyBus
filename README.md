# Party Bus

A Dalamud plugin for keeping track of the FFXIV venues you and your friends go to. Open it with `/partybus` or `/pbus`.

- **Venue list:** name, data center, world, district, ward and plot, plus notes. **Fill in from where I'm standing** reads the address when you're inside a venue or standing on its plot.
- **Green / red:** mark each venue **Like** or **Nope**. The dot shows your rating, or the crew's majority if you haven't rated it; the Crew column shows likes / dislikes.
- **Crew sync:** your list syncs with your crew through party chat, automatically when you group up. Sync lines are hidden for anyone with Party Bus. Only people on your crew list can change your list.
- **Open Now:** every venue advertising in Party Finder right now, even ones not on your list. Search it, add any of them to your list in one click, or copy the address.
- **Party Finder alerts:** open Party Finder and Party Bus reads the listings. Venues on your list are marked **OPEN**, and for green venues you get a chat alert (with a sound) and your party gets a "[Party Bus] Name is OPEN!" ping.
- **Share codes:** copy your list (or just the green ones) as a code a friend can import without being in a party.

Party Finder only shows your current data center, and Party Bus only sees listings while the Party Finder window loads them.
